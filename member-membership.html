QUICK ANSWERS

Membership FAQs

Helpful answers to common membership questions.

How can I check my membership status?

Check the Active Member status and plan details in the membership card section above. Confirm your actual status with the club if anything appears incorrect.

How do I review my payments?

Open the Payments section from your dashboard to review the payment information available in your member account.

Can I change my membership plan?

Available upgrades or plan changes depend on club policies. Contact member support to check your eligibility and any applicable charges.

When should I renew my membership?

Review the renewal date displayed in your membership details and contact the club before your plan expires.
